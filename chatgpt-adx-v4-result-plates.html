A3—Plain-Language Explanation

ADX does not generate an 8% distribution from ordinary stock dividends alone.

Its cash-flow mechanism combines:

portfolio dividends + realized capital gains + potentially return of capital.

The managed distribution policy targets approximately 8% of average NAV per year. The fund therefore relies substantially on appreciation in its equity portfolio being converted into realized gains.

That distinction matters. For the six months ended June 30, 2026, ADX generated only $11.63 million of net investment income, versus approximately $117.0 million of distributions. At the same time, however, it generated approximately $132.8 million of realized gains and another $176.2 million of unrealized appreciation.

The key economic test therefore is not ordinary-income coverage alone. For this type of equity CEF, NAV total return and realized gains are the more appropriate measures of distribution sustainability.

A18—Terms Used

NAV: Net asset value—the value of the fund's assets minus liabilities, expressed per share.

CEF: Closed-end fund. Shares trade on an exchange independently of NAV and can therefore trade at discounts or premiums.

NII: Net investment income—primarily portfolio dividends and interest less fund expenses.

Realized gain: Investment appreciation that becomes recognized when a security is disposed of.

Return of capital: A distribution classification representing amounts not currently characterized as investment income or realized taxable gain. ROC is not automatically destructive; its economic significance must be assessed alongside NAV and total return.

Managed distribution policy: A predetermined framework for distributing a specified percentage of NAV regardless of the precise amount of current portfolio dividend income.

Discount to NAV: The percentage by which a CEF's market price is below its underlying NAV.
