FINAL PRINCIPLE

The written report provides the depth, evidence and explanation.

The optional visual report provides the fast, polished, high-density summary.

The goal remains to answer:

"Is this investment moving because its underlying value has changed, or because the market's opinion of that value has changed?"

And when the investment is falling:

"Am I being offered essentially the same healthy income-producing asset at a better price, or is the lower price warning that the investment itself is becoming weaker?
